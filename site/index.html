How it works

Three beats. Then you’re looking at the sheet.

01

Point it at the products

Paste links, scan a barcode, or read a QR from the box. Two is enough. Three if the customer is torn.

02

The owl goes to work

We fetch the public product pages and send them to our API, which uses OpenAI (Gemini as fallback) to extract and group specs. Nothing from your camera roll is uploaded.

03

Read the difference

Tap a spec for a plain-language explain. Open a product. Export a PDF. History stays on this phone until you clear it.
