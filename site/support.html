FAQ

The comparison never finishes

Need two supported Canadian retailer links, and a network path to our API. Try two in-stock Best Buy Canada product pages. If it still fails, send the two URLs through the form below.

Camera or Photos permission

Only used to read a barcode or QR. URL compare works if you tap Don’t Allow. You can change this later in iOS Settings → SpecMatch.

A spec looks swapped or wrong

AI reads public pages; retailers format tables differently. Screenshot the row and send both product URLs so we can fix the mapping.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Clear search history removes on-device comparisons. For server logs that might still include a URL you submitted, send a privacy request below.

Does SpecMatch use AI?

Yes. Product page text is sent to OpenAI, with Google Gemini as a fallback. Photos from your library are not uploaded. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
